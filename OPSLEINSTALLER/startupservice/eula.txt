4. Privacy and Data Collection
OSPLE aims to respect user privacy and minimize unnecessary data collection.
Unless an individual project explicitly documents otherwise, OSPLE software is designed not to transmit personal information, telemetry, usage statistics, or other user data to remote servers by default.
4.1 Local Data Storage
Data generated or processed by OSPLE software is generally stored locally on the user's device or within the user's own network.
OSPLE developers do not automatically receive access to locally stored user data.
4.2 External Communications
Some OSPLE projects may communicate with external services when necessary for their functionality.
Examples may include:
* Software update checks.
* External APIs.
* Network services.
* User-configured integrations.
* Optional diagnostic or error-reporting services.
Where applicable, individual projects should document their external network communications and explain what information may be transmitted.
4.3 Diagnostic Information
OSPLE developers may request diagnostic information, error reports, or application logs when investigating bugs or technical problems.
Users are not required to voluntarily submit such information unless a separate, clearly disclosed arrangement applies.
Users should review diagnostic information before submitting it, as logs may contain personal information, system details, or other potentially sensitive data.
4.4 User Responsibility
Users are responsible for configuring their software, network connections, integrations, and external services according to their privacy and security requirements.
OSPLE cannot guarantee the privacy practices of third-party services that users independently choose to connect to.
5. Security and Software Limitations
OSPLE developers aim to develop and maintain software responsibly and to address reported bugs and security vulnerabilities when reasonably possible.
However, OSPLE does not guarantee that its software:
* Is completely free of bugs or vulnerabilities.
* Will operate without interruptions or errors.
* Will be compatible with every operating system or hardware configuration.
* Will remain compatible with every future software update.
* Will meet every user's security or operational requirements.
Users are encouraged to:
* Keep their software and dependencies updated.
* Obtain software from trusted distribution sources.
* Review software configuration before deployment.
* Maintain appropriate backups.
* Apply suitable security measures to their devices and networks.
Security-related issues may be reported to the appropriate OSPLE project maintainers through their officially designated communication channels.
